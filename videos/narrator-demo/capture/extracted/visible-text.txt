Narrator — a Mac menu bar app that reads a part of your screen aloud in a natural voice, follows new Claude replies as they arrive, and lets you ask questions about what's on screen. Everything runs on your Mac with a local Kokoro voice: no tokens, no API keys, works offline. Select an area with Option + Shift + 4, say what it's for, pick a mood (Calm, Warm, Serious, News, Story, Auto) and a voice (woman or man, US or UK). It reads sentence by sentence and highlights the line. Follow mode reads new Claude replies as they stream in. Jump (Option + Shift + J) continues from any sentence. Ask (Option + Shift + A) answers questions about what's on screen. Lumen, a moth librarian, lives on the Dock: flies to the icon of the app being read, its mouth and lantern follow the voice, shows tips on hover, can be picked up and dropped, sleeps when idle. Free and open source (GPL-3.0) at github.com/jain-eshan/narrator.
